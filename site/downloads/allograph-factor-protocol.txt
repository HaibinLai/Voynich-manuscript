Reversible FP / KT factor experiment v1 — freeze before model outcomes

Question: can statistical parameter sharing between f/p and/or k/t improve the
probability assigned to complete original words after paying every restoration
bit? A result is not evidence of phonetic identity, semantic identity or a
historical allograph. Motivated by already-explored confirm analyses and the
prior f57v observation; this is exploratory, not a novel discovery of variants.

Splits: train+legacy fit all counts; dev selects finite hyperparameters. Reserve
is never returned by corpus.load, fitted, scored or used for positive controls.
Confirm is already used in earlier project analyses. All dev configurations and
synthetic checks must be frozen and sent to root before a gated once-only
confirm pass. All 3 merge conditions × ZL/EVA, ZL/merged, IT/EVA are reported.
Main representation ZL/EVA, all other representations predetermined sensitivity.

Map: FP maps p→f; KT maps t→k; both does both. Merged representation additionally
maps cph→cfh (FP) and cth→ckh (KT). Units are explicit parsed units, not rendered
strokes; other identities remain. Ω consists of a-z, apostrophe, @000;..@999;
(EVA1027), plus ch/sh/cth/ckh/cph/cfh (merged1033). Exact quotient sizes:
EVA FP1026/KT1026/both1025; merged FP1031/KT1031/both1029. Canonical identity is
branch0; the other member of each pair is branch1. Composite pairs get separate
counts but share model depth/alpha. g is a unitwise, length-preserving map.

Probability: P_X(x|m)=P_Y(g(x)|m) × product over ambiguous positions j of
R_j(x_j|g(x),m). Each R is normalized over the complete preimage pair; unchanged
units cost0. P_Y is a normalized complete nonempty word distribution with EOS.
All original words remain possible. The restoration factors are conditionally
independent given the entire encoded quotient word plus known metadata. Future
quotient neighbors are allowed (already encoded); unrecovered original labels
are never conditioning inputs. Unknown legal original branches remain possible.

Quotient LM and raw baseline: order4, hierarchical count smoothing alpha100,
identical global/hand/word-position experts and 15 quarter-simplex mixture
weights. Word position = paragraph_first/line_first/other, using existing parser.
Each expert uses the same full train alphabet for that representation/map.
Missing hand/position expert falls back to global. Mixture is of independently
nonempty-conditioned probabilities. Train-unseen quotient identities each pay
log2(|Ω_Y minus train alphabet|) through a uniform UNK split; history uses UNK.
This is normalized but induces a different original-identity prior than raw
uniform escape; report escape components separately, no retrospective choice of
weighted escape. Training-outside Ω is an error, not silently omitted.

Restoration: separate counts for canonical pair f/k/cfh/ckh. Depth0 global pair
counts with symmetric1/2 prior; depth1 key(pair,hand,word-position,unit-role),
where unit-role=single/first/middle/last; depth2 additionally left/right quotient
neighbors with BOS/EOS. Every level uses (count + alpha*parent)/(N+alpha).
Finite grid depths0/1/2 × alpha1/5/20/100. One shared alpha/depth per merge and
representation, not separately optimized per pair. Dev selects mean complete
word restoration loss; ties lower depth then smaller alpha. Report best depth0
as a matched global-restoration sensitivity. LM weights and restoration choices
minimize additive separate dev losses, so independent choices equal joint grid
optimum. More parameter/search flexibility than baseline is explicitly admitted.
No additional pair, context, alpha, order, or representation search permitted.

Synthetic detection checks, before manuscript train/dev outcomes:
Five fixed seeds2026111741..45; each 8000 original words in 40 artificial pages,
200/page; train20/dev10/test10. Metadata hand alternates page H0/H1; every tenth
word is paragraph_first, other positions line_first every fifth, else other.
Known quotient lexicon fixed from seed,128 words, equal draw, each word has f/k
alias sites and short random abcd context. iid aliases Bernoulli .5; position
aliases Bernoulli .85 or .15 by hand/paragraph-first; difficult correlated labels
repeat the same pair decision at source/target positions separated by two units
(distance3, visible to raw4gram, invisible to independent quotient restoration).
Use all3 merges for each known mechanism, EVA only, same fit/dev budget. Exact
synthetic generation will be implementation-frozen before scoring. Report all
45 results, including poor/no detection; no threshold tuning. Positive controls
check reversible valid probability and restoration against known conditional
entropy; total predictive gain is not guaranteed. Correlated negative checks
whether lost original-label dependence is charged; not a classifier of meaning.

Mathematical/code checks: finite toy Ω includes unseen and composite alias
members. Enumerate every preimage of short quotient words; probabilities sum1,
reconstruction exact. Enumerate original words through length4 plus analytic
remaining prefix mass to verify complete normalization within1e−10. Nonempty
normalization, EOS, unknown branch, mixture and backoff checked independently.

Reporting: complete raw baseline bits, quotient bits (including quotient escape),
restoration bits, original complete bits, raw/quotient escape bits and unknown
counts. Primary paired contrasts are each merge vs raw baseline with selected
restoration; global-only sensitivity separately. Average page loss then leaf loss
then equal leaf mean.1999 leaf bootstrap:95% and conservative98.333% for3merges
within each representation; not project-wide exploration correction. Fixed seed
from SHA('allograph-factor-leaf-v1|representation'), same resamples across merges.

Save raw targets/input splits locally + deterministic gzip for publication; score
arrays/configs/protocol/source hashes. Independent scalar probability replay from
saved train/test artifacts, not new confirm load. New public reruns write only to
fresh output directories with fresh receipts; never overwrite frozen confirm.
