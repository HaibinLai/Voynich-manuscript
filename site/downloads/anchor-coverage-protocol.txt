Bax partial-table coverage audit, v1

This is an executability audit of the fourteen entries already recorded from
Bax (2014), pp.56-57, not a test of translation accuracy or a completed decoder.
The two non-reserve anchor images f29v and f68r3 were visually inspected before
this protocol. Their original/current transcription differences were also known
from the literature review. Do not call this visual audit blind or preregistered.
This file and the coverage implementation are hashed before aggregate coverage
is computed. No phonetic value, plant identity, spelling or rule is optimized.

Input: unchanged literature/candidate_claims.json, its fourteen literal EVA
patterns and symbolic phonetic alternatives. Use the existing atomic EVA parser,
including @nnn; as one explicit unit. Do not switch to a segmentation selected
for word matches. Since the paper does not specify a complete segmentation
algorithm, enumerate every path through the listed patterns (e.g. e+e and ee).
These are mapping paths, not a count of distinct pronunciations; an unspecified
V remains unspecified. Never replace V by a vowel chosen to match a name.

The m rule is conditioned on a sense-final/isolated interpretation that cannot be
determined from the unknown language. Report two bounds: conservative excludes
the m entry everywhere; optimistic allows it everywhere. Neither supplies the
missing historical condition. All other entries are used as the literal table
lists them, with no omitted q/p/t/ch/l and no deletion of initial gallows.

For each word, dynamic programming computes (1) whether at least one full path
exists; (2) the number of table-segmentation paths; (3) the number of symbolic
mapping paths after branching over the stated candidate values; and (4) the
minimum number of original EVA units left unexplained, allowing a one-unit
unknown fallback of cost1. Unknown units remain explicit. Zero unexplained
units means only that the table can assign symbols, not that the result is
correct, meaningful, grammatical or in any identified language.

Apply only to train+legacy and dev, reported separately, for ZL/EVA and IT/EVA.
Confirm and reserve pages are not returned to this audit. The known f15v anchor
is explicitly skipped in any example calculation. This new diagnostic is not
added to the already-frozen final reserve ten-comparison packet.

Report token and type denominators, full-coverable fraction, single-symbolic-path
fraction, multiple-path fraction and remaining-unit fraction. All optimistic and
conservative results are shown. No p-value or translation-success percentage.
Keep unresolved cases, source differences and source-image provenance. Modern
paper page images remain private; public crops, if any, come directly from Yale
with exact original image hashes and pixel coordinates.

Implementation checks: compare dynamic programming with independent exhaustive
path enumeration on short words and all explicitly listed table overlaps;
ensure unknown symbols are never dropped, extend paths through the entire word,
and verify m conservative/optimistic ordering. Freeze before corpus statistics.
