This notice applies to catalogue-derived material, not as a claim that all repository code or all official image files have the same licence. The medieval work and its modern catalogue transcription are different sources. Full modern English translations/commentary and the complete modern catalogue are not reproduced here.

Changes from the catalogue: the historical Latin quotations were extracted, retained with editorial marks and source offsets, and arranged by folio and role. Derived name fields retain the original joint quotations and known missing entries. No spelling, u/v, i/j, inflection or synonym normalization is asserted as historical truth. Chinese explanations, geometric observations and qualifications were added by this research project. The two leaves belong to the same manuscript.

Public projections preserve scientific fields and source hashes. Only explicitly identified private local-output path prefixes are replaced by the descriptive marker private-output/; that marker is not a published download directory. See projection_receipt_v1.json for original and public hashes. Official full images, interrupted downloads and private crops are not included in this package; their source URLs and dimensions remain available in the reports.

The published reports are completed qualification stages. They do not certify complete glyph transcription, authorize a Voynich translation, or contain real name-in-description matching scores. The f2r P07 small block and catalogue coverage remain unresolved at this snapshot. Later bounded investigations preserve these older records.
