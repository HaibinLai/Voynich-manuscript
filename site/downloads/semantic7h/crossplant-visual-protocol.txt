Cross-page illustration comparison protocol v1
AI-assisted geometric observer, not a historical-botanical or palaeographic expert.
Scope: the 12 source_numbered_locator pair hypotheses supplied in plant_pairs.json and label_support.json, including fragment 110 with multiple text positions. Frozen before image comparison or any crossplant lexical-result inspection.
Source hypothesis is prior literature, not ground truth. Source grades remain attributed to Zandbergen/Petersen. No species naming or transcription, reading or lexical scoring in this review. Label-support text was visible during permitted input reading; therefore no claim of full text blindness.
Use the author's numbered diagrams only to locate the intended object. Inspect Yale official manifest canvas metadata and original image before comparing visible parts. Save URL, original dimensions, SHA256 and approximate original-pixel object region; rectangles are reading/object regions, not precise contours.
Compare visible shape only: main axis orientation, branching/attachment, root silhouette, leaf lobing/outline and arrangement, terminal shapes and colour placement. State similarities AND differences; parts absent from the small fragment are missing evidence, not a match. Drawing style and repetition can produce superficial similarity.
Outcome per pair: suitable_for_partial_shape_comparison, limited_or_ambiguous, failed_localization, or deferred_canvas_conflict. None is semantic gold or proof of the same plant. Keep alternative target objects and source grouping unresolved when needed. Record adjacency of text without copying glyphs or inferring label identity.
Do not drop negative/failed pairs. Do not tune inclusion from lexical results. Same source folio/leaf and repeated target folio/object are dependent, not independent confirmations. #110 is one grouped source hypothesis with multiple candidate text positions, not several independent tests.
Do not inspect the disputed f100v #136 -> f90v2 target canvas until parent resolves OID 1006236 (Yale label 90r). The deferral remains in report.
Allowed writes: this visual/crossplant directory only. Do not alter prior frozen geometry, prior calculations or root research files. Do not republish modern redraws or full source articles; retain source links and only necessary identification metadata. Official manuscript image assets are primary evidence.
