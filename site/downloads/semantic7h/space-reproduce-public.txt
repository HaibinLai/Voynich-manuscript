Space-bridge public numerical replay v1

Requirements: Python 3.9 or newer, standard library only. No network, GPU,
credentials, absolute workstation paths, or unpublished metadata are needed.
From a clean checkout containing the explicit public allowlist, run:

python3 research/research_next/semantic7h_20261118/design/space_bridge/replay_public.py --output ../space-bridge-replay-new

Choose a NEW output directory OUTSIDE the checkout. Existing directories are
refused. The entry point verifies all allowlisted hashes and historical input,
result, and image-review metadata hashes before reconstructing the experiment.
It imports the frozen independent parser, numerical core, and schema adapter;
it does not import the primary scorer. It compares the fresh reconstruction
with the archived independent reconstruction and every primary opportunity,
hit, position, denominator, rate, NA, contribution, union, inventory, and summary.
The output contains reconstructed.json.gz, summary_16.json, and
replay_receipt.json. PASS requires zero differences. Output gzip bytes can
differ from the historical gzip serialization; numerical content is compared.

The canonical public package inventory is public_repro/allowlist_v1.json.
That manifest excludes itself to avoid circular hashing. Subsequent validation
and delivery receipts are separately listed in the public release manifest.
Only listed files are proposed for release. Do not recursively add this folder:
audit_numeric_freeze_v1.json contains local absolute path keys and remains an
unchanged private provenance record. The public counterpart is the distinct
public_repro/audit_numeric_freeze.public.json, which changes only five path
keys to repository-relative paths. public_repro/path_projection_v1.json records
both hashes and the exact transformation. No numerical output or frozen source
was changed to make the projection.

Use replay_public.py for reproduction. Historical audit.py, audit_numeric.py,
audit_compare.py, and make_report.py retain their original behavior and may
write historical paths or expect private freeze metadata; do not invoke their
command-line entry points in an archival checkout. They are retained as source
provenance, not as the safe public interface. The original primary scorer and
tests are included for inspection. The eight previously published report,
audit, and projection files are byte-locked by
public_repro/published_eight_lock_v1.json.

Scope: this is replay of an already exposed, exploratory fixed experiment,
not new blind confirmation. It retains all 11 single-label cases and the
excluded three-word #110 group receipt. ZL/IT and strict_space/packet_tokens
are all reported. Exact, adjacent-forward, adjacent-reverse, and gap-two
forward rules and their separate exact unions remain distinct. Unknown slots,
recorded tags, within-locus continuity, atomic characters, and common bench
seams follow protocol_v1.txt. Encoded barriers cannot guarantee that every
interruption visible in the manuscript was transcribed. Image-review metadata
are checked, not independently re-annotated; historical plant relationships
are conditional hypotheses, not an expert object-identity gold standard.

The replay does not modify or reinterpret the findings: no target joined-word
hit, and no target adjacent-forward union gain, occurred in the fixed cases.
This excludes a narrow proposed spelling mechanism for these queries; it is
not a translation or a general rejection of meaningful manuscript language.
