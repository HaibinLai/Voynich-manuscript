Treat whitespace and the ordinary catalogue punctuation . , ; : ! ? and quotation marks " ' ‘ ’ “ ” as boundaries, after uncertainty annotations have been separated by the editorial ledger. A known run or block is never joined through an unknown barrier or into another block.

Measurement and summaries
Follow protocol_review_v2.txt: Xij is exact complete sequence presence within one known run of one block. A known match gives 1 even when other material is unknown. Without a match, any unknown body content gives measurement NA; complete defined content without a match gives 0. An undefined name, undefined group or undefined block boundary is a definition failure, not a binary missing cell; stop the corresponding complete analysis and report that failure. Current declared unknown segments/blocks have fixed group identities; their content is unknown rather than their indexed existence.
Compute T_s=sum_i X_i,(i+s)%12 / 12 for all offsets. Main delta is T0 minus mean of T1..T11; auxiliary delta subtracts mean of all twelve. Keep their 12/11 relation and distinct names. No NA=0, reduced denominator, label deletion, tie-breaking, phase-as-independent-sample or p value.
With unresolved binary cells, no complete main point estimate or exact rank is claimed. Provide fixed-denominator phase intervals and linear coefficient bounds on each delta. These are outer bounds under unconstrained cell completion; shared source uncertainty makes them potentially non-sharp. Do not claim every endpoint or rank can jointly occur. A strictly positive lower bound would support an advantage under all relaxed completions, conditional on C; a bound crossing zero does not decide the sign. Exact ranks and full tie sets are only reported for complete matrices. Report every cell with its block/run/offset occurrence witness or reason for 0/NA, not just summaries.

A positive result does not show that nonlinguistic copying is impossible; a negative or inconclusive result does not disprove semantic association.
