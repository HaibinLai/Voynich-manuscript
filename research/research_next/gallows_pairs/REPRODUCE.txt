从仓库根目录执行，均使用标准库。
python3 research/research_next/gallows_pairs/analyze.py --output /tmp/gallows-pairs-rebuilt
python3 research/research_next/gallows_pairs/balanced.py /tmp/gallows-pairs-rebuilt /tmp/gallows-pairs-rebuilt/balanced.json

freeze.json 验证事前协议和上轮输入的字节。revision.txt 记去重口径页字段和跨版汇总调整。analyze.py 写全框、恰1边及局部格。balanced.py 是主结果后对照，理论期望用人工枚举核对。将独立复算写入新目录：
python3 research/research_next/gallows_pairs/audit.py --run research/research_next/gallows_pairs/saved --output /tmp/gallows-pairs-audit-rebuilt
