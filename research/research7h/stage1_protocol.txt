Stage 1 frozen protocol — 2026-11-17
Purpose: test whether prior 20-page positional and similarity signals generalize to previously unscored manuscript leaves, then evaluate minimal predictive models. No semantic decipherment claim.

Corpus: published ZL3b primary; IT2a sensitivity. Same preprocessing as study20: drawing interruption is a boundary, uncertain commas split, ambiguous/illegible tokens are missing slots, ligature braces removed, extended codes atomic. EVA and merged-unit representation separately. P0/P1/Pc/Pr/Pt only for primary linear prose; Pb/labels/circles excluded. '=' and Pt append to previous physical line with recorded provenance. New @ block or explicit paragraph marker resets paragraph context. Paragraph-start status comes from explicit <%> only; never inferred from first known token. Missing first slots stay missing.

Split: all known 20-page sample leaves plus f1/f3 become legacy/train. Remaining physical leaves grouped (all f85/f86/Rosette sections together) and assigned by deterministic seeded metadata-stratified round robin to train/dev/confirm/reserve with proportions 40/20/20/20. Metadata stratum is majority illustration/hand/Currier label per leaf; no outcomes used. Small strata may lack confirm coverage. Split manifest frozen before aggregate results. Reserve not scored in this stage. 'Held out' means withheld from fitting and scoring, not that source contents have never been machine-loaded or glimpsed.

Four primary confirmatory tests, Holm family across four:
G1: first-token EVA k/t/p/f enrichment vs same-line other slots, only explicit paragraph first lines.
G2: same statistic for non-paragraph-first lines with established paragraph context.
R1: exact-repeat rate for adjacent interior tokens (exclude first and last slots of each physical line), vs within-line interior-token permutation.
R2: distinct edit-distance-one rate for the same pairs and null.
Page effects equal-weight; pages lacking relevant valid pairs/slots omitted for that test. All token-missing masks retained. For G tests shuffle valid tokens inside each eligible full line. For R tests keep line first/last fixed and shuffle valid interior tokens. 1999 replicates for primary ZL/EVA, fixed seed 2026111701; other versions/representations 399. Two-sided empirical deviation from null mean, add-one p. Report permutation reference intervals, not effect CIs.
Report per page, grouped by I/H/L, including null/negative outcomes and denominators. No inferential subgroup claims without separate correction. Supplement R2 with length-exact within-line interior shuffles and full-line/page-pair conditional analytic expectations. Separate exact vs nonidentical one-edit, never combine after seeing direction.

Additional descriptive measures (exploratory, no confirmatory p): lagged exact/near rates at within-line distance 1–5 with matching available pairs; paragraph first vs following line profiles; removal of a leading gallows as dictionary attestation. These cannot alone identify mechanism or language.

Predictive models (freeze before confirm): train on train+legacy; hyperparameters on dev only; evaluate once on confirm. Character-within-token iid/bigram/trigram/fourgram with interpolated backoff, beginning/end markers and unseen-symbol bucket. Condition optional models on known hand/section, and paragraph-start vs other position. Token unigram and short-range cache mixtures (window 1/4/8/16, weights 0/.05/.1/.2/.4) evaluated in original vs shuffled context, with masks/line resets. Primary scores are mean negative log2 probability per observed token/character, with page/leaf summary, not fluent generated passages. Exact architecture details must be saved before first confirm scoring. These are prediction benchmarks, not causally complete generation accounts.

Pre-outcome parsing clarification: '=' is relative to the immediately preceding source locus. If that source locus is an excluded margin label, the prose is a new physical prose line aligned to that label; do not join it to the previous retained prose line. This occurs on f11v/f49v/f76r. Pt joins its prior prose line. The initial 20-page study did not contain these affected folios.

Known limitations: inherited metadata may be uncertain; full-book images not independently audited; Currier labels themselves partly encode text statistics; folds can be spatially complex; manuscript leaf sequence need not equal original order; cross-token sequential comparisons avoid crossing line boundaries. P-value sensitivity and transcription disagreement reported. No monetary API use or outside messages.
