Reversible glyph-factor experiment — public reproduction

All nine exploratory manuscript contrasts and all 45 synthetic checks are kept.
The declared result is limited to statistical parameter sharing. KT global-only
restoration is negative in all three representations; conditional restoration is
necessary for its positive gain. This is not a decipherment or allograph proof.

Use Python 3.9+ and NumPy. Matplotlib is needed only for rebuilding the figure.
Preserve the research7h / sources / study20 layout and the frozen relative paths.
From this folder:

  python3 public_reproduce.py preflight
  python3 public_reproduce.py calibrate --seeds 2026111741 --output /tmp/allograph-calibration-new
  python3 public_reproduce.py replay --scope all --output /tmp/allograph-replay-new

Omitting --seeds reruns all five seeds, each with iid, position-conditioned and
correlated aliases, all three merges. Every run freshly fits train counts and
selects only from the frozen development grid. It compares resulting gains with
the original saved results. No manuscript corpus is loaded by calibration.

Saved-result replay validates gzip/raw hashes, copies train/targets/configs/scores
to the NEW output directory, then independently reconstructs scalar probabilities
and binary restoration tables. It does not import the corpus parser, reopen
confirm or touch reserve. --scope manuscript/synthetic can narrow replay, but all
results are in the public package. The new output directory must not already
exist and may not be inside this frozen package. Fresh execution receipt and
verification reports are written there. Original evidence is never overwritten.

The frozen run.py records the original execution. Its existing confirm marker
intentionally prevents a second scoring pass. The public wrapper has NO fresh
manuscript-confirm or reserve mode. To inspect raw saved records locally, run
restore_data.py; it checks both gzip and original bytes and never overwrites a
changed original. report.py can render from saved runs alone; rebuilding compressed
archives after any originals are restored requires all36 saved input/target files.

Validation: all45 synthetic plus all9 manuscript results independently replayed;
normalization/unknown identity costs and roundtrip toys passed. Public calibration
reran one seed across all3 mechanisms × all3 merges and reproduced every gain.
Public saved-data replay reran all54 cases in a new directory. Exact known-pair
truth in replay_synthetic.json is used for calibration interpretation: the frozen
run's one-pair truth field is a half-full-word reference, not the exact selected
pair's realized loss. No predictive score or parameter choice was changed.

Model capacity and tuning budgets differ: both word LMs select15 style mixtures,
but the quotient factor additionally selects among12 restoration configurations.
model_complexity.json reports train-only context/count-cell sizes, not effective
statistical degrees of freedom. Complete costs, recovery bits, escape bits and
per-leaf uncertainty are retained. The reserve corpus was never returned, fitted
or scored in this experiment; shared parser hashing/reading is distinct from
model exposure to a reserved page.
