Page-level H scope clarification, appended before opening reserve
2026-11-17. Original protocol, freeze, models and preflight remain unchanged.

The operational hand condition in the frozen primary packet is the IVTFF PAGE
header H value. The parser used by all original experiments does not propagate
inline <@H=...> overrides. An audit of allowed train+legacy/dev metadata found
one affected training page, f115r: page H=@, source loci1-12 H2, loci13-45 H3.
The old models treat @ as a separate observed category, not as an identified
sixth scribe. Therefore the primary packet tests prediction conditional on those
frozen page-level labels; it is not a complete adjustment for real per-line
Davis hand attribution. This qualification applies to previous descriptions of
known hand or hand-controlled prediction.

There are456 retained ZL words on f115r (128/328 by explicit H2/H3 labels) and444
IT words (120/324). These counts are affected training populations, not a bound
on how much any score may change. Global and position data are unchanged. The
paragraph-prefix family does not consume H; the other families may be affected.
Prior independent score replays continue to verify the original implementation.

A separate metadata_sensitivity packet is being frozen before reserve opening.
It changes only explicitly recoverable H assignments, keeps every old dev
parameter/mixture choice, and recomputes train+legacy counts. It will report all
eight affected contrasts in three representations (24) with old/new per-word,
page and leaf values, plus unchanged paragraph checks. It is descriptive
robustness, with no additional search, p-values or confidence-family claims.
It does not replace the primary ten-contrast packet or rewrite its outputs.

After both packets pass their recorded preflight/confirm review, root will bind
this addendum and the sensitivity freeze in the reserve execution gate. The
sensitivity must run on the same saved reserve pages regardless of whether the
primary result is positive or negative; it is not optional reporting chosen
after inspecting scores. Any inability to execute it is reported as incomplete.
The old page-H and corrected inline-H results must both remain available.

No reserve scoring has occurred at the time of this addition. The source-format
and propagation audit is in section_hand_audit/metadata_scope_audit.txt and its
machine-readable companion. This note changes interpretation scope, not any
numeric result, input hash, predeclared target or model parameter.
