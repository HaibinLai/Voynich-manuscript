Frozen final reserved-page adapter and its seen-confirm preflight

The preflight_v1 directory is a calibration of the new adapter on ALREADY-SEEN
confirm data. It is not the final reserve result. Read mode/split in summary.json
and the execution receipt before citing any number. The initial publication of
this package occurs before reserve_opened.json exists.

Preserve the repository's research / sources / study20 / research7h structure.
From the repository root, a fresh confirm preflight can be reproduced with:

  python3 research/research7h/final_reserve/score.py preflight \
    --output /tmp/voynich-final-preflight-new --workers 2

The output directory must not exist. It verifies every frozen code/configuration
and source hash, reconstructs only the original fixed training counts, checks
per-word scores against historical confirm arrays, reproduces six position-null
arrays, and produces all30 comparisons and9 descriptive sensitivities. It does
not return reserve pages to any model. The shared source parser still traverses
the public source file; do not equate this with file-level blindness.

Some frozen logical JSON inputs are distributed as deterministic JSON.gz.
score.py accepts this fallback only after hashing the decompressed bytes against
the original frozen identity. No historical hash or selection receipt is edited.

preflight_audit.py and preflight_reference_audit.py are additional independent
saved-data audits. They were run on the original source layout; before invoking
them on the compressed public snapshot, restore the referenced copy/allograph
JSON archives using those packages' hash-checked restore tools as needed. Their
saved audit receipts are included. They are independent probability/context and
aggregation checks, not semantic evidence.

The reserve entry point is deliberately gated and limited to the original
research window. It is not a general-purpose public decoder. Do not invent a
gate, refreeze the old study under a different clock, or silently overwrite
saved results. A future study needs its own explicit protocol and new outputs.
Once saved reserve arrays are published, probability/aggregation replay can be
done from those records without pretending to rerun a new blind experiment.
