Final reserved-page replication protocol v1
Prepared 2026-11-17, before any reserved-page model scoring.

Purpose and scope
Re-evaluate the following fixed predictive explanations on the project's 22-page,
11-leaf reserve partition. This is a replication of selected exploratory results,
not a decipherment attempt with a known semantic answer. No model is selected,
refitted, calibrated or revised using reserve outcomes. Counts remain fitted to
train+legacy only; all hyperparameters are the already frozen dev choices. Dev
and confirm are not added to training. This deliberately includes previously
negative contrasts, and all three predeclared transcription/unit representations.
The separate fixed-substitution experiment retains its own confirm protocol and
does not use reserve in this final predictive packet.

Exposure and independence
The reserve partition is b8773a853896e08a03eea857668ee08825076babf1b93b7939461f70023db2c0
(SHA-256 of split_manifest.json). Its raw public files were downloaded previously,
and the shared parser traverses all source records before filtering returned pages.
Metadata and layout were used to make the original leaf split. Bax's f15v anchor,
Timm's default f103v seed, and published full-book aggregate studies were seen.
See reserve_exposure_log.txt; never describe the project as wholly blind.
No reserved-page per-word model score or reserve-specific statistic has yet been
computed. All 22 pages remain primary; do not discard exposed or inconvenient
pages. A fixed auxiliary summary omits leaves f15 and f103 together, reports all
contrasts, and is labelled an exposure sensitivity, not a replacement primary test.

Representations and parsing
ZL_EVA is primary; ZL_merged and IT_EVA are fixed sensitivities. Use unchanged
corpus.py, build_corpus.py and study20/analyze.py, with uncertain commas split,
alternative/unreadable words excluded as missing slots, and existing paragraph /
physical-line joins. Labels and circles are not silently treated as prose.
All retained target slots and all skipped/missing slots are counted. Exact source,
parser, model, configuration and probability-table hashes are locked in freeze.json.
No manual correction is applied after viewing reserve outcomes. A discovered bug
requires a dated amendment, preservation of the original results, and explicit
rerun labelling; failure to support a hypothesis is not a reason to amend.

Global-only restoration for each merge is a mandatory descriptive sensitivity.
No new grid, alphabet, pair, restoration depth or smoothing parameter is allowed.

Meaning of the score
These are conditional complete-word prediction costs, in bits per retained
original normalized word. They condition on known transcription layout and hand;
the cost of conveying that metadata is not included. Copy sources must be earlier
in reading order under the already fixed rule; no future word content is allowed.
Missing/unknown slots retain prior reset and eligibility rules. Unknown words are
not dropped merely because they are poorly predicted. A conditional compression
gain is neither plaintext, a word meaning, proof of a language, nor an end-to-end
archive compressor. Different task populations (all words / interior pairs /
paragraph-first words) are never ranked by raw bits against each other.
The allograph models have additional restoration parameters and search choices;
their predictive gain does not establish phonetic or historical identity.
Paragraph-start annotations may themselves partly reflect conspicuous first
glyphs; the layout gain must not be interpreted as an independent causal effect.

Aggregation and uncertainty, frozen before reserve outcomes
Within each eligible page take the mean paired word loss; within each leaf take
the equal mean of its eligible pages; then weight eligible leaves equally.
Report pooled word means and every page/leaf value separately. A page/leaf with
no eligible targets is absent for that contrast, with its absence reported, not
assigned zero. This unified page-then-leaf convention differs from the earlier
paragraph-factor report's pooled-within-leaf convention; retain both historical
results and clearly name the final convention.
Use 99,999 whole-leaf bootstrap draws with a deterministic seed from
SHA256('final-reserve-bootstrap-v1|representation|sorted eligible leaf names').
The leaf names are comma-joined; use the first eight digest bytes interpreted
little-endian, NumPy default_rng, integer draws, and quantile method='linear'.
Contrasts with the same representation and eligible leaves share draw indices.
Report ordinary 95% intervals and 99.5% intervals for the ten primary ZL/EVA
contrasts (Bonferroni tail levels for a nominal 5% family budget). Also report
99.833333% intervals across all 30 representation/contrast combinations, so a
cross-representation statement cannot silently select an easier interval.
These are approximate percentile cluster-bootstrap intervals with at most eleven
leaf groups and possible within-book dependence, not exact simultaneous coverage
or an estimated probability of a hypothesis. No bootstrap tail fraction is called
a null-hypothesis p-value. Report leave-one-leaf-out ranges to expose dominance.
With zero eligible leaves report no estimate; with one eligible leaf report its
mean without a confidence interval or leave-one-leaf-out range. The 99,999 draws
do not increase the number of independent manuscript leaves.
The exposure sensitivity uses the same fixed models and all contrasts, with
descriptive per-leaf means only; no second opportunity to claim a positive test.

Position-specificity auxiliary check
Reuse the existing frozen copy and perturbation implementation, without retuning:
within each previous source line, permute eligible word identities while keeping
slots, missing values, horizontal channel and target text fixed. Main strata use
exact unit length, auxiliary strata the original length bins 1-3/4-6/7-9/10+.
Each source line receives one shared permutation per replicate, even if reused by
many targets. Run 1,999 draws per representation/stratum with frozen hash-derived
seeds. Save all null gains, actual identity movement and changed-source-set rates.
Seed key is 'final-reserve-position-v1|representation_name|exact_or_coarse', first
eight SHA256 digest bytes little-endian, NumPy default_rng. Quantiles use linear
interpolation. Existing confirm seeds are used only by the adapter preflight.
Report original vertical gain, null mean, their difference and reference Monte
Carlo tail area. Apply Holm to all six reference tail areas as one auxiliary
family. The interpretation requires conditional exchangeability and is a V-channel
intervention, not a coherent whole-text shuffle or proof/disproof of historical
copying. Do not fold these tail areas into bootstrap intervals.

Execution gate and reproducibility
1. Freeze this text and an allowlist of all code/configuration/model inputs.
2. Run the new final scoring adapter only on already-seen confirm records and
   compare every saved per-word cost with historical arrays. Boundary may differ
   only by the explicit common target escape term; paragraph aggregation may
   differ only by the stated convention. Independently inspect normalization,
   allograph restoration, and past-only copy source checks already archived.
3. A second agent reviews the protocol/adapter while reserve remains unopened.
4. Write one explicit internal gate receipt after preflight passes. In the final
   stage, write reserve_opened.json before returning any reserve records to model
   code. Model configurations and training inputs must be frozen first; workers
   deterministically reconstruct counts from those fixed training inputs without
   tuning. Each model family runs in its own Python process, avoiding collisions
   between identically named models.py/reference_models.py modules. Execute the whole
   predeclared packet once. This version deliberately has no automatic resume
   entry point. If interrupted or failed, preserve every partial output and mark
   the packet incomplete. A later strictly missing-output continuation requires
   a dated implementation amendment bound to the same immutable inputs; never
   overwrite or hide partial results, change seeds, or silently rerun scores.
5. Independently replay scores/aggregation from saved records without re-reading
   or retuning on reserve. Save original arrays, hashes and deterministic gzip
   of contexts. Publish positives, negatives, missing eligibility and limitations.
6. The original research window still ends 2026-11-17 16:50:25 UTC. Incomplete work
   is labelled incomplete; no smaller post-result experiment is substituted.
