This package contains support counts, not label/body word-match results.

Read 设计审查.txt first. No model or semantic result is claimed.

To restore the three deterministic gzip archives:
  python3 restore_inventories.py

In the project research tree (sources/ and study20/ are two levels above here):
  python3 inventory.py
  python3 design_summary.py
  python3 verify.py

inventory.py reads split_manifest first and streams only explicitly allowed
train/legacy/dev folio payloads through normalization. The complete source file
is traversed and hashed; disallowed-page word payload is not parsed or returned.
Output records contain unit lengths and missing-slot status, not word strings.
No routine computes exact/near label/body lexical hits.

design_summary.py can also be run from the restored length-only inventories;
it does not need manuscript transcription text. verify.py independently checks
allowed locus lengths/missingness, conditional donor metadata and existing
frozen body denominators. Its toy strings are synthetic, not manuscript quotes.

These are design-support counts. Locus-to-drawing object correspondence has not
been independently established. Do not interpret candidate counts as independent
sample size or the strength of a semantic association. No new images or reserve
outcomes were examined. No website or final_reserve files were modified.
