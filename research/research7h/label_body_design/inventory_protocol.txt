Label/body design inventory — no lexical hit experiment
2026-11-17

Authority: parent requested a read-only feasibility audit. Do not alter site,
frozen experiments, split_manifest or final_reserve. No confirmation/reserve
text is to be returned or tokenized; no new image viewing or semantic anchors.

Selection: read split_manifest.json first; keep exactly its train, legacy and
dev folio identifiers (154 pages, 74 leaf groups). Do not add other surfaces of
an allowed foldout leaf: the manifest was itself built from prose-bearing pages.
The initial type-count reconnaissance preceded this inventory specification;
no label/body exact or approximate lexical matching was computed.

Stream ZL3b-n.txt and IT2a-n.txt by page headers. Skip disallowed pages before
parsing locus payload. The existing tokenizer is imported without invoking the
whole-corpus parser. It splits uncertain spaces, removes markup and marks a
whole token containing alternatives/unknown signs as missing. Store lengths,
slot counts and locus identifiers only, not word strings. EVA primary; merged
unit lengths are also retained for design sensitivity. No matching/edit code,
corpus fitting, semantic identification, random shuffling or p-values.

Image-associated locus types: La,Lc,Lf,Ln,Lp,Ls,Lt,Lz. Inventory L0 and Lx
separately; do not silently treat them as object labels. Nonexistent ! loci are
excluded. Body: frozen P0,P1,Pc,Pr,Pt inventory; Pb is separately reported rather
than added to the frozen body definition. Circle/radius C/R are not prose.
Locus count is not independently established object count. @ means the relative
position is unrelated to the prior locus or not easily described (also used for
the first page item); it is not proof of a new independent drawing label. +,=,~ and *
are layout markers, not a unique object-identification scheme. No automatic
merge of adjacent L loci is attempted. Report whole-locus readability, multiword
loci and the complete-singleword subset, with and without @. The @-only
subset is merely a layout-marker sensitivity, not a higher-confidence gold set.

Design support only: for each retained label slot, count possible other label
pages/leaves under the same analysis pool (train+legacy or dev), fixed known
page H and I, and exact label unit length; require a different physical leaf.
Also count controls with the same subtype and with the same quire Q. These are
covariate support counts, not label/body lexical matches. Unknown/mixed H is not
a sixth scribe and is excluded from conditional control strata. Do not estimate
an outcome even on pages with adequate support.

Additional design alternative: keep a label fixed and use any allowed prose
page (including one without labels) with the same H/I/pool and a different leaf.
Count support with a positive number of words of that label length, and then
with equal numbers of such length opportunities. This is length availability,
not word-form identity; it is a different estimand from wrong-label replacement.

Both-version coverage uses locator/type presence and slot completeness, never
label/body word identity. Equal slot indices do not establish alignment when
word boundaries differ; the clean singleword subset is reported separately.

Outputs: a reproducible inventory script, allowed-page list, per-page counts,
per-locus lengths, matching support and short design report with direct prior
art. No local absolute paths in publishable outputs. This is a feasibility
description, not a new frozen efficacy test or a guarantee of exchangeability.
