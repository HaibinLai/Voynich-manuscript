Coverage scope clarification, after original aggregation and before publication.
No original rule, input, denominator or outcome is changed by this note.

The fixed corpus.load call returns prose-locus pages only. Its default tokenizer
marks an entire unreadable or alternative-reading token as None. coverage.py
counts only the remaining nonempty explicit-unit words. The reported token/type
fractions therefore refer to retained prose words in train+legacy and dev, not
every handwritten mark, label, circular text, ambiguous token or all manuscript
pages. The three targeted image anchors are a separate visual audit; labels are
not silently added to the numerical coverage denominator. A filtered unknown
word is different from an explicit known EVA unit that Bax's partial table does
not cover: the latter remains in the denominator and is charged as unexplained.

The m-excluded / m-allowed pair brackets only the treatment of that conditional
table entry within the literal inventory. It is not a confidence interval or a
general bound on every possible future extension of Bax's hypothesis. The input
corpus and proposal are both public, and the visual checks were informed by prior
transcriptions; this is not a blind trial or a percentage of text deciphered.
