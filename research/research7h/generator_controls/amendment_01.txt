Amendment 01 — 2026-11-17, after inverse-code audit, before morphology outcome scoring

The official UNAMBIGUOUS=True rule prevents bigram ciphertext from colliding
with unigram ciphertext. It does not prevent distinct prefix/suffix splits from
representing different bigrams. Exhaustive table inspection found 202 accepted
table-pair encodings with a nonunique decoder output, out of 18913 accepted
table-pair encodings; several encodings can produce the same ciphertext.
The first generated Latin test consequently did not have a unique direct inverse.

Retain the official variant unchanged and report ambiguity. Add a separately
named strict-unique variant: extend the official rejected-glyph set with every
bigram ciphertext whose official basic decoder has multiple readings. Keep the
tables, card weights and all other rules unchanged. This deterministic change
is motivated solely by inversion, not by any manuscript-statistic fit.
This variant is our adaptation, not the published Naibbe implementation.
Require 100% unique token decoding and exact normalized plaintext recovery for
the strict-unique positive control. Candidate inclusion and unique decoding are
reported separately for the official version. The existing spacing-deletion
variant remains separately reported and is never assumed to be uniquely reversible.

Sample-size clarification: if a permitted manuscript pool has fewer than 8000
valid tokens, compare it in a separate panel where every generator is truncated
to that same smaller N. Do not choose N from fit quality. The 8000-token generator
minimum and original 20-seed schedule remain unchanged. This addresses corpus
availability, not outcome matching. All confirm/reserve outcomes remain excluded.
